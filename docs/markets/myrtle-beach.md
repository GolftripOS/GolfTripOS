# Myrtle Beach / Grand Strand — Market Profile

> GolfTripOS launches as a **Myrtle Beach-only** app (decided 2026-09-30). Once this market is perfected, the same template is copied to the other top-30 markets. Source of truth: the W4 task page in Notion. App data: `market-profiles.js`.

**Coverage:** About 60 miles of coast, from Pawleys Island and Georgetown, SC up through Brunswick County, NC (Calabash, Sunset Beach, Ocean Isle, Holden Beach).

## At a glance

- **Golf courses:** 90+
- **Rounds per year:** 3M+
- **Annual visitors:** 18.2M (2024)
- **Visitor spending:** $13.2B direct (2024)
- **Lodging:** ~98,500 rooms, 400+ properties
- **Metro population:** ~413K

## Why groups go

- Known as the "Golf Capital of the World." The first course opened in 1927, so 2027 is the 100th anniversary of Myrtle Beach golf.
- Built for group golf trips: huge course supply, stay-and-play packages, and condos sized for foursomes and bigger groups.
- Nearly all lodging has a full kitchen or kitchenette. Multi-bedroom condos and golf villas are the go-to for groups.
- Canada is the top international market, celebrated every March during CAN-AM Days.

_Overall tourism dipped about 3% in 2025 (weather, social media coverage, economic pressure). The city is investing in Ocean Blvd improvements. Golf demand is a separate segment from summer beach tourism._

## Pick your area (vibe by zone)

| Zone | Towns | Vibe | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Central Strand** | Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside | Party | The high-energy center with the most tourists: bars, nightlife, Broadway at the Beach, Barefoot Landing and shows. Best for bachelor parties and younger buddy groups. |
| **North Strand** | Little River, Calabash, Brunswick County NC | Relaxed | More laid-back, without the party crowd. Good value and known for Calabash-style seafood. Suits relaxed or older groups. |
| **South Strand** | Murrells Inlet, Pawleys Island, Georgetown | Premium | Upscale and quiet, with many of the championship courses. Suits premium or serious golf groups. Murrells Inlet's MarshWalk has a lively dinner-and-drinks scene. |

## Who is there

- Local residents skew older: the metro had the fastest-growing 65+ population in the US in 2024 (+6.3%, +22% since 2020), and 65+ residents are over 25% of the population.
- Visitors vary by season: families dominate summer; spring and fall bring golf groups, retirees and Canadian snowbirds; bachelor parties and young buddy trips come year-round, mostly to the Central Strand.

## Getting there

| Airport | Approx. drive | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **MYR** — Myrtle Beach International | ~10 min | Primary airport. Record 3.84M passengers in 2024 (+14%). 59 nonstop destinations, the most in SC. Allegiant, American, Avelo, Breeze, Delta, Frontier, Southwest, Spirit, Sun Country, United. |
| **ILM** — Wilmington, NC | ~1.5 hr (~45 min to NC-side courses) | Best alternative for groups staying on the North Strand or NC side. |
| **FLO** — Florence, SC | ~1.25 hr | Small regional airport with limited service. |
| **CHS** — Charleston, SC | ~2–2.5 hr | Bigger hub, sometimes cheaper fares. Closest to the South Strand. |
| **CLT / RDU** — Charlotte / Raleigh-Durham | ~3.5 hr | Major hubs for groups willing to fly in cheap and drive the rest. |

Historically a drive-in destination (I-95, the Carolinas, Virginia, Ohio Valley and the Northeast). Many groups mix flyers and drivers.

## When to go

| Season | Months | Golf demand | Pricing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spring peak | Late Mar – Apr | Highest | Top courses ~$150–200, budget ~$70–80 | Book early; tee times go fast. |
| Fall peak | Oct – early Nov | High | Near peak | Great weather and a popular buddies-trip window. |
| Summer | Jun – Aug | Low for golf, high for families | Low green fees, high lodging | Humid with afternoon thunderstorms. Play early tee times. |
| Value season | Late Nov – early Jan | Low | Cheapest overall; hotels ~$50–60/night | Highs in the upper 50s to low 60s; possible frost delays. |

## What this means for the app

- **Area picker:** let organizers choose North, Central or South Strand by vibe (party, relaxed or premium) before picking courses.
- **Season-aware suggestions:** the AI assistant should factor in peak and value windows and warn about frost delays and summer storms.
- **Group lodging first:** 3–4+ bedroom condos and golf villas are the main lodging product here.
- **Mixed arrival logistics:** support MYR vs. ILM vs. CHS arrivals plus drive-in groups.
- **Launch hook:** the 2027 "100 years of Myrtle Beach golf" story lines up with the Feb 1, 2027 launch.
